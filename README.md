# FibreCraft — Fibre Manufacturing Business Website

A modern business website for a fibre manufacturing and design factory that specializes in creating custom fibre products, including God statues, decorative gates, party entrances, portraits, sculptures, and other artistic structures.

## 📌 About the Project

This project is a professional website developed for a factory owner and his manufacturing business.

The factory designs and builds a wide variety of products using fibre-reinforced materials, combining creativity, craftsmanship, and durable construction.

The website provides the business with a professional online presence and allows potential customers to explore the factory's work, learn about its products, and get in touch for custom orders and enquiries.

## ✨ Products & Services

The factory creates a variety of custom fibre products, including:

- 🛕 God Statues & Religious Idols
- 🚪 Decorative Gates & Entrances
- 🎉 Party & Event Entrances
- 🖼️ Fibre Portraits
- 🎨 Custom Sculptures
- 🏛️ Large-Scale Fibre Structures
- 🔧 Custom Fibre Designs
- 📐 Made-to-Order Products

Customers can request customised designs based on their requirements, dimensions, theme, and intended use.

## 🎯 Website Goals

The main goals of the website are:

- Showcase the factory's craftsmanship and completed projects.
- Present the available products and services.
- Build trust with potential customers.
- Provide a professional digital identity for the business.
- Make it easy for customers to enquire about products.
- Showcase previous work through a visual gallery.
- Generate leads and custom-order enquiries.

## 🖥️ Website Sections

### Home

An attractive landing page introducing the business, highlighting featured products, completed projects, and providing clear calls-to-action for customers.

### About Us

Information about the factory, its experience, craftsmanship, capabilities, and approach to custom manufacturing.

### Products

A collection of the different fibre products manufactured by the factory, organised into categories for easy browsing.

### Gallery

A visual showcase of completed projects, including statues, gates, entrances, portraits, sculptures, and other custom fibre work.

### Custom Orders

Information about the customisation process and how customers can request products based on their specific requirements.

### Contact & Instant Lead Actions

- **Fixed 3D Floating Action Dock**: Fixed circular buttons on every page (bottom-right) with tactile 3D depth, bevel highlights, and dynamic shadows for instant phone dialing and WhatsApp chat/reference upload.
- **Reference Concierge Lead Generator**: Interactive multi-step estimator that compiles user project specs directly into WhatsApp message threads with zero database storage.

## 🛠️ Architecture & Tech Stack

- **Architecture**: 100% Client-side Frontend / Standalone Static Platform (No database, no backend server overhead, zero maintenance).
- **Core Technologies**: HTML5, Modern Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+).
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS + Custom High-End Atelier CSS (`assets/css/luxury.css`).
- **Motion & Smooth Inertia**: GSAP 3 (GreenSock), ScrollTrigger, and Lenis Inertial Scroll.
- **Icons**: Lucide Icons.
